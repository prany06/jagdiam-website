About

JAGDIAM is a Tokyo-based diamond and jewelry wholesaler with over 25 years in the trade. We supply GIA and CGL certified diamonds, colored stones, and fine jewelry to dealers, jewelers, and private clients across Japan and internationally. Whether you're sourcing a single stone or building a collection, we provide personalized service and competitive pricing.

What We Offer

Certified Diamonds

GIA & CGL certified single stones, all shapes and sizes, D–Z and fancy colors.

Parcels & Lots

Volume supply of 50–100+ stones, ideal for manufacturers and dealers.

Fine Jewelry

Rings, earrings, and custom pieces available for retail and wholesale buyers.

Colored Stones

Ruby, sapphire, emerald, and other precious gems sourced globally.
